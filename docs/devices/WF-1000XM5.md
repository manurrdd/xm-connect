# WF-1000XM5

Not verified here: this is read from a session recorded on firmware 6.1.0 and published by
SonyHeadphonesClient. It is enough to check the parsers against a real v2 device, and not enough to
claim the app works on one.

Answers on the v2 service. Frames arrive on both data types, `0C` and `0E`, and the second
support-function list comes in on `0E`, so a client that only reads `0C` misses half the device.

```
recv     01 00 03 00 30 18 00 00
recv     07 00 2E 10 FF 12 FF 13 FF 14 FF 15 FF 16 FF 17 FF 23 FF 25 36 11 FF …
recv     67 17 01 00 01 00 14
recv     57 00 A0 06 14 0B 0A 0D 09 0C
recv     23 09 00 00 64 00 64 64
recv     23 0A 35 00 1E
recv     D1 D1 00 01 10 SIDETONE_SETTING 18 SIDETONE_SETTING_SUMMARY
recv     D7 D1 00 01
recv     D1 D2 00 01 12 MULTIPOINT_SETTING 29 MULTIPOINT_SETTING_SUMMARY…
recv     D7 D2 00 00
```

Forty-six announced functions in table 1, each with a priority byte, which is the paired layout v2
uses and v1 does not. Noise control announces `6B`, so the wire variant is `17`. The equalizer is
six values on a custom preset. Battery comes as left/right and case, both with thresholds
appended.

Two general-setting slots, sidetone and multipoint. Both differ from v1 in ways that matter:

- The capability reply carries a setting type before the string format, which v1 does not have.
- On is `00` and off is `01`, the opposite of v1.

Reading a v2 slot with the v1 layout yields nothing rather than a wrong answer, which is how the
divergence was found.
