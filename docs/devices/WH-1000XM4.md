# WH-1000XM4

Answers on the legacy service, so it speaks the v1 command table. Captured with `xmprobe`:

```
recv     01 00 70 00
recv     07 00 17 71 38 62 F5 81 51 A1 E1 E2 D2 F6 D1 F4 F3 39 12 13 11 30 C1 14 22 21
recv     61 02 02 00 01 02 00 14 01 14
recv     67 02 00 02 00 01 00 14
recv     51 01 06 15 0C 00 00 10 00 11 00 12 00 13 00 14 00 15 00 16 00 17 00 A0 00 A1 00 A2 00
recv     57 01 00 06 0A 0A 0A 0A 0A 0A
recv     11 00 5A 00
```

Protocol version `0x7000`, reported as a 16-bit value with no table-support bytes after it.

## Noise control

The capability reply (`61`) gives `ncSettingType 02`, `ncStep 0`, `asmSettingType 01`, and two
ambient modes with twenty steps each: `00` normal and `01` focus on voice.

`ncSettingType 02` is the value to write back. Two published implementations hardcode `01` instead,
which this capture rules out for the WH-1000XM4.

The state reply (`67`) repeats both setting types, so a client that echoes what it reads needs no
per-model table.

Writing noise cancelling is confirmed on the device:

```
sent     68 02 11 02 02 01 00 00
recv     69 02 01 02 02 01 00 00
recv     67 02 01 02 02 01 00 00
```

The write carries `effect 11`, adjustment complete, and the device answers with `effect 01`, on.
Both mean the same thing, and a reader that only checks for a non-zero effect handles either.

## Equalizer

Six values, Clear Bass first, `0A` for a flat band: the same layout and the same −10 to +10 scale
the v2 devices use. Only the inquiry byte differs, `01` here against `00` on v2.

The capability reply announces six bands, twenty-one steps, and twelve presets: `00`, `10` to `17`,
and `A0` to `A2`. Every one of them comes back with a zero-length name, so the names have to come
from the shared preset ids instead of from the device.

## Settings behind the announced functions

Two general-setting slots, each announcing its own subject as an enum key rather than a label:

```
recv     D1 D1 02 13 TOUCH_PANEL_SETTING 00 01 00
recv     D7 D1 01 00
recv     D1 D2 02 12 MULTIPOINT_SETTING 1A MULTIPOINT_SETTING_SUMMARY 01 00
recv     D7 D2 01 01
```

Both are boolean slots (`01` in the value reply), and for this family `00` is off and `01` is on,
the opposite of the on/off encoding v2 uses elsewhere. The values match what Sony's phone app
shows, which confirms the polarity.

Writing one takes four bytes, `D8 slot settingType value`. The three-byte form the generated
structs describe is acknowledged and changes nothing, so the setting type the device reported has
to come back with the value. Upscaling answers `E7 02 00 01`: automatic
mode, on.

The system inquiries answer as well, and are not decoded yet:

```
recv     F7 03 00 01     playback control by wearing
recv     F7 04 01 10 00  auto power off
recv     F7 05 00 00     speak-to-chat
recv     F7 06 01 00     assignable controls
```

## Battery

Battery is reported as a single level: neither the left/right (`15`) nor the case (`18`) layout is
announced, as expected for an over-ear model.
